📅 Scheduling

Appointment booking, provider availability, waitlists, and automated reminders. The logistics engine that connects patients to the right provider at the right time.

What This Context Does

Scheduling manages the when and where of healthcare delivery. When a patient calls to book an appointment, this service finds available time slots based on the requested provider's schedule, books the slot, and sets up automated reminders.

It also manages waitlists — if the preferred time slot is taken, the patient can join a waitlist and automatically get offered the slot if a cancellation occurs. This reduces no-shows and maximizes provider utilization.

In plain English: This is the hospital's appointment book and front desk scheduling system. It knows who is available when, prevents double-booking, and reminds patients about upcoming visits so they don't forget.

Domain Model

Appointment Aggregate Root

Links a patient, provider, date/time, and visit type. Supports lifecycle: Scheduled → Confirmed → Checked-In → Completed or Cancelled/No-Show.

PropertyTypeDescription
AppointmentIdint (PK)Unique identifier
PatientIdint (FK)Who the appointment is for
ProviderIdint (FK)Which doctor/nurse
AppointmentDateDateTimeScheduled date and time
DurationintMinutes (15, 30, 60)
TypestringNew Patient, Follow-Up, Urgent, Telehealth
StatusstringScheduled, Confirmed, CheckedIn, Completed, Cancelled, NoShow

Availability Entity

Provider's available time slots by day. Generated from the provider's schedule in Provider Management. Blocks out lunch, meetings, PTO.

Waitlist Entity

Queue of patients waiting for a specific provider/date. When a cancellation occurs, the first person on the waitlist gets an automated offer.

Reminder Value Object

Automated reminders sent via SMS or email. Default: 24 hours and 1 hour before appointment. Configurable per patient preference. Immutable once sent.
